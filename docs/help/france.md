# France

France's chart is drawn only when it is asked for: while its box is ticked, or when the command line names it, `--nations:fr` (or `fra`) alone or with others, as in `--nations:ca,us,fr`. Its amounts are in euros (€), its rates in percent a year. Figures before 1999 are French francs converted to euros at 6.55957, as the ECB and Eurostat publish them in national currency. This page says what the chart holds for France; the page of each curve, linked below, gives its sources in full.

## What the chart draws

At the start it draws GDP, debt and interest on the right axis, and the 10-year bond yield on the left; the policy rate, inflation, mortgage rates, the price index or the population when they are ticked.

- [TTM nominal GDP](?help=gdp), from 1980: four-quarter sums of Eurostat's quarterly national accounts at current prices.
- [Aggregate public debt](?help=debt), from 1995: general government consolidated gross debt, the ECB's year ends to 1999, Eurostat's quarters from 2000.
- [TTM interest outlays](?help=interest), from 1995: four-quarter sums of general government interest payable, Eurostat's quarters.
- [Balance of payments](?help=payments), from 1999: Eurostat's quarterly balance of payments, not seasonally adjusted: the current account's credits and debits, or exports and imports of goods and services; the quarters after its last from its monthly figures.
- [By level](?help=levels): the debt and interest of central government or of local government instead of the total, from Eurostat's figures by subsector: the debt quarterly from 2000, the interest yearly from 1995 (each year's at its last day), since Eurostat's quarterly interest is for general government alone. France has no state level; its social security funds are no level of the chart.
- [Bond yields](?help=yields), from 1960: the 10-year alone, the OECD's long-term interest rate on FRED, a monthly average drawn across its month. The chart's End is then the first day of the last month published, where its last average is drawn as a point. No other terms, no real yields, and so no yield spreads.
- [Policy rates](?help=policy-rates): the euro area's, the ECB's main refinancing rate from 1999-01-01; the overnight market's rate, EONIA from 1999-01-04, €STR from 2019-10-01. None before 1999.
- [Inflation](?help=inflation), from 1956-01: the 12-month rise of France's consumer prices, Eurostat's harmonised index from 1996 and the national index before it, scaled to it where it begins; the rises from 1997 are the harmonised index's alone.
- [Mortgage rates](?help=mortgages), monthly from 2000-01: the ECB's rates on new loans to households in France for house purchase, fixed for over 1 to 5, over 5 to 10 and over 10 years. All three are ticked at the start. The variable rate (a variable rate or one fixed for up to a year) is offered beside them.
- [Per capita](?help=per-capita): divided by the population the Population box draws.
- [Inflation adjusted](?help=constant-prices): at the consumer prices of one month, by the same index, from 1955.
- [Price index (CPI)](?help=price-index): Eurostat's harmonised index of consumer prices for France from 1996-01, 2025 = 100, and before it, from 1955-01, the national index as the OECD published it, scaled to it; unadjusted; no seasonally adjusted index is offered.
- [Population](?help=population): Eurostat's quarterly figures from 1980.
- [Latest values](?help=latest-values): no quotes during the day for France; its yield is a monthly average, so the chart ends at the last month published.

## Its own controls

Under France's chart in the window, and under "France only" on the page, are the controls that set its chart alone: the policy rates and mortgage terms it draws, its levels of government, and the [limits of its axes](?help=limits): Min and Max on the left, Top and Bottom on the right, in € unless another currency is named by its code (USD20t). On the command line each is written after France's prefix, `--fr:` or `--fra:`, as in `--fr:max:8`.
